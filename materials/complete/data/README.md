# Synthetic training study

`training-study.csv` is a fictional dataset created for this workshop. It
contains no real people or confidential information.

Variables:

- `participant_id`: synthetic identifier
- `group`: `comparison` or `intervention`
- `training_hours`: synthetic hours of training completed
- `baseline_score`: score before training
- `followup_score`: score after training

The dataset may be reused under the CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication.
Its values are designed for teaching and must not be interpreted as evidence
about a real intervention.
